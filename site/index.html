Recursive Eco-Improvement

explorations in adaptive alignment

If we cannot know alignment is possible without building it, that unknowability is itself the reason to stop. A place to try to be a good actor in all of this, even not knowing if it will matter.

The bad-actor problem and the alignment problem ask for opposite remedies: one pulls countries apart, the other pulls them together. These pages try things about AI safety and ethics in the open: words you can draw as cards, sources you can check, films, and small games where you move something and watch what the move costs. A working title and a work in progress. Corrections are welcome.
