Recursive Eco-Improvement

explorations in adaptive alignment

A thing that improves itself still has to adapt with the ground it stands on, or change that ground. These pages hold that idea three ways: words you can draw as cards, sources you can check, and small games where you move something and watch what the move costs. A working title and a work in progress. Corrections are welcome.

The grammars

Practice and record, kept apart

Everything here is a grammar: a deck, a sequence or a course you can open, fork or correct. Practice holds readings and games; Record holds dated, attributed sources. A practice claim is kept apart from a historical one.

One channel of recursive.eco

recursive.eco is an open platform built on grammars: symbolic systems people make meaning with. This site is one channel of it, held in git, so every grammar here is plain JSON you can read, fork or correct.
