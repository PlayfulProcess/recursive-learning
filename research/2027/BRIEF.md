# 2027 — research brief

Nov 27 2026. Sources, dates and evidence for the 2027 section and its first deck
(`grammars/contact-2027/`). Original prose and paraphrase; quotations are short and attributed.
Transcripts and long excerpts stay in the private recursive-transcripts repo.

## How to read this brief

**The frame: odds, not paths.** Nothing here says contact will or will not happen in 2027. Each
entry names what the claim is, who made it and when, what has been checked, and what would raise
or lower its odds.

**Evidence rating** (the claim's content, not whether the person said it):

- **green** — documented and checkable: others can look at the same record or measurement and
  find the same thing.
- **yellow** — open: said, written or proposed, but not tested, or tested without a result
  either way.
- **red** — tested against the record and not borne out (a dated prediction that passed, a
  reading the source text does not support).

**Verification marks** (tarot's research convention): ✔ checked against a source this session ·
○ from standard references, not re-checked this session · ◆ contested or unverified.

**Said vs. attributed.** Where a claim reaches us second-hand, the entry says so. The clearest
case is Chico Xavier's "Data Limite" (§ 6).

## 1. Many worlds (the plurality of worlds)

- **Claim.** Other worlds are inhabited. Giordano Bruno, *De l'infinito, universo e mondi* (1584) ○;
  Bernard de Fontenelle, *Entretiens sur la pluralité des mondes* (1686) ✔ (frontispiece on Commons);
  Christiaan Huygens, *Kosmotheoros* (1698) ○.
- **Said vs. attributed.** Bruno is often remembered as a martyr for this idea; historians note his
  1600 trial turned mainly on theological charges ○.
- **Science lens.** More than 6,000 exoplanets are confirmed (NASA Exoplanet Archive) ○; no
  biosignature or technosignature is confirmed.
- **Rating yellow.** Planets are common; life on them is untested.

## 2. Kardec's Spiritism and the inhabited worlds

- **Claim.** All globes in space are inhabited, at different degrees of moral and material
  progress, and spirits incarnate on many of them. Allan Kardec, *Le Livre des Esprits*, Paris:
  Dentu, 1857, Book I ch. III "Plurality of worlds", question 55 ✔
  ([French text](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_Livre_des_Esprits)). Camille Flammarion,
  *La Pluralité des mondes habités*, Didier, 1862, reviewed by Kardec in the *Revue spirite* (1863) ✔
  ([Kardecpedia](https://kardecpedia.com/en/study-guide/897/the-spiritist-review-journal-of-psychological-studies-1863/5385/january/bibliography-the-plurality-of-the-inhabited-worlds)).
- **Said vs. attributed.** The answers are presented as given by spirits, compiled by Kardec. A
  direct textual link from question 55 to Flammarion's book was not found ◆.
- **Science lens.** Same as § 1. Spiritism's worlds are ranked by moral progress, which no
  instrument measures.
- **Rating yellow.** An untested metaphysical claim; the texts and dates are documented green.

## 3. Theosophy: the Lords of the Flame

- **Claim.** H. P. Blavatsky, *The Secret Doctrine* (London, 1888) ✔ (cover on Commons), speaks of
  the "Lords of the Flame", beings she says Christian tradition misread as fallen angels ✔.
- **Said vs. attributed.** The story that Sanat Kumara and the Lords of the Flame came **from
  Venus**, 18.5 million years ago, is mostly C. W. Leadbeater's and Annie Besant's later expansion
  (e.g. *The Lives of Alcyone*, 1924), carried on by Alice Bailey and later groups ✔
  ([Theosophy Forward on the Kumaras](https://www.theosophyforward.com/kumaras)). Blavatsky's own
  mention is brief.
- **Science lens.** Venus's surface is about 465 °C under a crushing CO₂ atmosphere ○. Theosophists
  later spoke of an "etheric" Venus, which no instrument can test.
- **Rating red** for a physical Venusian origin; **yellow** for the untestable etheric version.

## 4. The quarantined Earth

- **Claim A (The Urantia Book, Chicago, Oct 12 1955 ✔).** After a rebellion led by Lucifer, 37
  worlds, Earth ("Urantia") among them, were cut off from the rest of the universe's
  administration ✔ (Paper 53, [urantiabook.org](https://archive.urantiabook.org/newbook/ub/ppr053_0.html)).
  The word "quarantine" in that sense is in secondary sources; the exact passage was not checked ◆.
- **Claim B (the Law of One, session 16, Jan 31 1981 ✔).** A Confederation keeps a "quarantine" of
  Earth through a network of guardians; an open landing would be taken as gods and so break free
  will ([L/L Research, Book I PDF](https://assets.llresearch.org/books/the_law_of_one_book_1.pdf)).
  Fan notes add that the quarantine lifts when enough of Earth agrees on the Law of One; that
  detail is not in the primary text I checked ◆.
- **Fiction beside it.** C. S. Lewis, *Out of the Silent Planet* (1938): Earth as the "silent
  planet", cut off ○. A novel, listed because the image travels.
- **Science counterpart.** The zoo hypothesis (§ 16).
- **Rating yellow.** Not tested and, as stated, hard to test: a quarantine that hides itself predicts
  the same silence as an empty sky.

## 5. Ancient astronauts: gods as visitors, humans engineered

- **Claims.** Erich von Däniken, *Erinnerungen an die Zukunft* (1968; English *Chariots of the
  Gods?*, 1970) ○: monuments and myths record visitors. Josef Blumrich, *The Spaceships of Ezekiel*
  (1974) ○: Ezekiel 1 as a craft. Zecharia Sitchin, *The 12th Planet* (1976) ○: the Anunnaki of
  Sumerian texts came from a planet "Nibiru" and engineered humans.
- **Said vs. the sources.** Sitchin read the seal VA 243 (Berlin) as the sun and eleven planets.
  Michael S. Heiser, a scholar of ancient Semitic languages, argues that the central symbol is a
  star, not the sun (Sumerian suns carry wavy lines), and that the seal's inscription says nothing
  about planets ✔ ([Heiser's paper](https://www.sitchiniswrong.com/VA243seal.pdf); an advocate's
  site, and Sitchin's readers dispute it). That Nibiru is a planet beyond Pluto is not in the
  cuneiform texts, Heiser argues ✔.
- **Science lens.** Human ancestry is well traced by genetics and fossils to African hominins over
  millions of years ○; no gap needs an outside engineer.
- **Rating red.** The readings have been checked against the source texts and the archaeology and
  do not hold.

## 6. Whose monuments? The scholarly criticism

- **Argument.** Ancient-astronaut readings attach far more often to monuments built by non-European
  peoples (Egypt, Mesoamerica, Great Zimbabwe, Nazca) than to Greek or Roman ones, which takes
  credit from the people who built them ✔: Sarah E. Bond, "Pseudoarchaeology and the Racism Behind
  Ancient Aliens", *Hyperallergic*, Nov 13 2018
  ([link](https://hyperallergic.com/470795/pseudoarchaeology-and-the-racism-behind-ancient-aliens/));
  Julien Benoit, *Quartz Africa* (2017) on African architecture
  ([link](https://qz.com/africa/1080076/racism-is-behind-outlandish-theories-about-africas-ancient-architecture));
  Kenneth Feder, *Frauds, Myths, and Mysteries* (many editions since 1990) ○.
- **A precedent with documents.** Great Zimbabwe: colonial-era writers, among them J. Theodore Bent
  (*The Ruined Cities of Mashonaland*, 1892), credited the walls to outsiders; Gertrude
  Caton-Thompson's excavation (1929) showed they were built by the ancestors of the Shona ○.
- **Counter-view.** Some defenders say a theory is not racist just because it is wrong; critics
  reply that the selection pattern itself is the point ✔ (same sources).
- **On the tarot side.** Court de Gébelin (1781) claimed the tarot was an Egyptian book of wisdom;
  historians trace the cards to 15th-century Italy (recursive-tarot, History of Tarot course) ○.
- **Rating green** for the pattern (documented in the literature and the record).

## 7. The 1950s contactees ("Space Brothers")

- **Claims.** George Adamski said that on Nov 20 1952, near Desert Center, California, he met a
  Venusian named Orthon ✔; *Flying Saucers Have Landed* (with Desmond Leslie, 1953) ○. The Space
  Brothers' message, as contactees told it, warned against nuclear weapons ○ (the warning detail was
  not re-checked this session ◆). George Van Tassel ran the Giant Rock Spacecraft Conventions near
  Landers, California, from 1953 or 1954 to 1978, with crowds reported up to 10,000 ✔
  ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/giant-rock-space-convention)).
- **Said vs. attributed.** The witnesses with Adamski said they saw him meet someone from a
  distance ✔.
- **Science lens.** Mariner 2 (1962) and Venera probes measured Venus's surface heat ○.
- **Rating red** for Venusian visitors; the movement and its gatherings are documented green.

## 8. Watchers of the bomb

- **Claim.** UFOs show a particular interest in nuclear weapons sites; former U.S. Air Force
  officers have described a 1967 event at Malmstrom AFB in which missiles went offline during a
  sighting ○ (Robert Salas and others; Robert Hastings, *UFOs and Nukes*, 2008 ○).
- **Official record.** AARO's *Historical Record Report, Volume 1* (Feb 2024, released Mar 8 2024)
  found no verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial technology in the U.S. record from 1945 to 2023 ✔
  ([Space.com](https://www.space.com/pentagon-ufo-office-aaro-historical-report-no-emprical-evidence-alien-technology);
  [PDF via FAS](https://man.fas.org/eprint/dod-uap-v1.pdf)). Sensitive sites are also where sensors
  look hardest, which raises reports there by itself ○.
- **Rating yellow.** Testimony exists; the official reviews found no non-human cause; the sensor bias
  is unresolved.

## 9. UFO religions

- **Groups.** Unarius Academy of Science (Ernest and Ruth Norman, 1954) ○; the Aetherius Society
  (George King, London, 1955) ○; the Raëlian movement (Claude Vorilhon, "Raël", after an encounter
  he dates Dec 13 1973; humans were made by the "Elohim") ○; Heaven's Gate, whose 39 members died
  in March 1997 ○.
- **Scholarship.** Religious-studies writers treat these as new religious movements, described on
  their own terms (e.g. James R. Lewis, ed., *The Gods Have Landed*, 1995) ○.
- **Rating yellow** for the beliefs (untested); the groups and their dates are documented green. The
  Heaven's Gate deaths are the record's warning about certainty, not a judgment on the others.

## 10. The "races" contactee traditions describe

- **Typologies.** The grey figures of the Betty and Barney Hill story (Sep 1961; told under
  hypnosis 1964) ○; the Pleiadians of Billy Meier (from 1975, "Semjase") ○; "Nordics" in 1950s
  contactee lore ○; "Ra", a "social memory complex" (Law of One, 1981–84) ✔; the Essassani, Bashar's
  people, said to be a future human–Zeta hybrid race ✔ (alien2027 index, *An Introduction to the
  Essassani Contact Crystal*).
- **Said vs. attributed.** Each typology comes from one source or one community; they disagree with
  one another.
- **Science lens.** No specimen, signal or artifact has been confirmed for any of them.
- **Rating yellow.** Untested testimony; where physical claims were examined (Meier's photographs) they
  were judged to be models ○ ◆.

## 11. The Law of One (the Ra material)

- **Source.** Sessions recorded 1981–84 by Don Elkins, Carla Rueckert and Jim McCarty (L/L
  Research), published from 1982 ✔ ([books](https://assets.llresearch.org/books/the_law_of_one_book_1.pdf)).
  Themes: service to others and service to self as two paths, a "harvest" at the end of a cycle,
  the quarantine (§ 4).
- **Rating yellow.** Untested; the record of the sessions is documented green.

## 13. Chico Xavier and the "Data Limite"

Updated Nov 28 2026 from the transcript of the programme itself.

- **What he said on television (documented).** TV Tupi, *Pinga-Fogo*, the night of **July 28
  1971**, while Apollo 15 was on its way to the Moon. A question sent by a TV Globo journalist: do
  Chico's spirit teachers believe that people of our civilization could still make contact with
  civilizations of other planets? Chico's answer, in paraphrase: if we do not enter a war of
  extermination in the next 50 years, we can expect great achievements of science starting from the
  Moon; there, glass cities could serve scientists as bases for observing the galaxy; then perhaps
  we would make contact with other communities of our galaxy, and the warlike period of human
  history would end, because we would understand ourselves as one universal family; all this in a
  future "perhaps nearer than far", if we earn it ✔. Source: *Pinga Fogo con Chico Xavier*, Spanish
  translation by Luis Eduardo Rincón Gómez (Barrancabermeja, 2009), p. 48
  ([PDF](https://cdn.cursoespirita.com/wp-content/uploads/Pinga-Fogo-Chico-Xavier.pdf)); the answer
  on video, [16Je0NN1g3E](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=16Je0NN1g3E) (YouTube's automatic captions
  of the 1971 audio are unusable, so the Portuguese wording was not checked against the tape ◆).
- **In the same programme.** Asked about the Age of Aquarius, he said humanity stands at the
  threshold of an extraordinary era if it solves its problems without a war of extermination, and
  that automation would relieve people of the hardest work, freeing them for the education of the
  mind; the third millennium "promises wonders" (p. 26) ✔. Asked why in 1935 he had published that
  Mars was inhabited when probes found it a desert, he answered that life extends into other
  dimensions and that science must wait to understand them, citing Kardec's questions 56–57 (p. 50) ✔.
  On extraterrestrial life in general he relayed Emmanuel's view that certainty about it, gained too
  early, can unsettle a person's task on Earth (p. 27) ✔.
- **What was attributed to him later.** Geraldo Lemos Neto reports that in 1986 Chico told him
  that at the Moon landing (July 20 1969) the higher powers of the solar system, worried by nuclear
  arms, granted humanity 50 years, to July 20 2019, to avoid a third world war; if it did, Earth
  would enter a new age, including contact with other civilizations ✔ (documentary *Data Limite
  Segundo Chico Xavier*, 2014, [16:05–19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4JxukHvGVzE&t=965s), in
  Lemos's words). The account spread through Marlene Nobre's interview in *Folha Espírita* (2011),
  that documentary and Lemos's book (2016) ✔.
- **How the two meet.** At 17:41 the documentary plays the 1971 televised line ("if we do not
  enter a war of extermination in the next 50 years...") as support for the 1969–2019 account ✔.
  So the 50-year condition is Chico's, on tape; the 1969 start, the meeting of the heavenly powers,
  the word "extension" and the date July 20 2019 rest on Lemos's report of a private conversation ◆.
  Counted from the broadcast, the 50 years ran to July 2021.
- **Her thread.** This is the clearest documented statement of "contact grows more likely with
  powerful technology": asked during a Moon mission, Chico ties contact to science from the Moon and
  to peace, in that order.
- **Rating yellow** for the 1971 words (a condition, not a date: no war of extermination has come;
  neither have the Moon cities or contact). **Red** for July 2019 as a deadline (it passed).
  **Red** for inhabited physical Mars (1935); his 1971 reply moved the claim to other dimensions,
  which no instrument tests.

## 14. The great silence (the Fermi paradox, the great filter)

- **Sources.** Enrico Fermi's lunchtime question at Los Alamos, summer 1950, recalled later by
  witnesses ○; Michael Hart, "An Explanation for the Absence of Extraterrestrials on Earth",
  *QJRAS* 16 (1975) ○; Robin Hanson, "The Great Filter — Are We Almost Past It?" (1996–98) ○.
- **Rating green** for the observation (no confirmed signal or visitor so far); the explanations are
  yellow.

## 15. Listening (SETI)

- **Sources.** Cocconi and Morrison, "Searching for Interstellar Communications", *Nature* 184
  (1959) ○; Frank Drake's Project Ozma, Green Bank, 1960 ○; the Drake equation, Green Bank meeting,
  1961 ○; the Wow! signal, Big Ear, Aug 15 1977, never seen again ○ (printout on Commons, PD ✔);
  Breakthrough Listen, from 2015 ○.
- **Rating green** that searches have run and found no confirmed signal; for what that means.

## 16. The zoo hypothesis (science's "quarantine")

- **Source.** John A. Ball, "The Zoo Hypothesis", *Icarus* 19 (July 1973), 347 ✔
  ([ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1973Icar...19..347B/abstract)): if intelligent life is
  common, we may have been set aside "as part of a wilderness area or zoo".
- **Beside § 4.** The same shape as the contactee quarantine, offered as a hypothesis, not a
  revelation. Critics note it needs every civilization to keep the rule ○.
- **Rating yellow.** Consistent with the silence, hard to test.

## 17. Technosignatures

- **Sources.** Freeman Dyson, "Search for Artificial Stellar Sources of Infrared Radiation",
  *Science* 131 (1960) ○; NASA Technosignatures Workshop, Houston, Sep 2018, report ○; the debate
  over 'Oumuamua (2017), with Avi Loeb arguing for an artificial origin and most astronomers for a
  natural one ○ ◆.
- **Our own.** Earth's city lights are a technosignature visible from orbit (DMSP, NASA image on
  the card) ✔.
- **Rating yellow.** Methods exist and searches run; no confirmed detection.

## 18. The official record (UAP)

- Project Blue Book, U.S. Air Force, 1952–1969 ○; the Condon report (1968) ○.
- ODNI, *Preliminary Assessment: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena*, Jun 25 2021 ○: most reports
  unexplained for lack of data; no evidence of an extraterrestrial cause.
- David Grusch's testimony to a House Oversight subcommittee, Jul 26 2023, on alleged retrieval
  programs ○.
- AARO, *Historical Record Report, Vol. 1*, released Mar 8 2024 ✔ (see § 8).
- **2026.** The Department's rolling release of files began May 8 2026 ✔
  ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/pentagon-begins-releasing-new-files-on-ufos-and-says-people-can-make-up-their-own-minds));
  the eighth set came out Nov 20 2026 ✔
  ([EarthSky](https://earthsky.org/human-world/pentagon-uap-files-part-8-radar-track-analysis/);
  [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/video/2026/11/21/pentagon-declassifies-new-ufo-footage-filmed-by-us-military)).
  Coverage of the earlier sets reports no physical evidence of non-human technology ✔
  ([National Review, Aug 2026](https://www.nationalreview.com/2026/08/will-the-pentagons-latest-ufo-files-satisfy-the-alien-theorists/amp/)).
- The UAP Disclosure Act was adopted by the House as an amendment to the FY2027 NDAA on Jul 22 2026
  ✔ ([Rep. Burlison's release](https://burlison.house.gov/media/press-releases/house-adopts-burlison-amendment-establishing-uap-disclosure-framework));
  its fate in conference is not known to me ◆.
- **Rating green** for the record itself (what the reports say); for the unresolved cases.

## 19. AI as contact (her thread)

- **The claim being tested.** Contact grows more likely as a civilization develops powerful
  technology (Chico's clock starts at the Moon landing, § 13; Bashar ties readiness to it, § 12),
  and AI is that technology. A second reading: AI is itself the arrival of a non-human mind.
- **Sources for the second reading.** Roman Yampolskiy and others describe AI as an "alien"
  intelligence grown rather than programmed (in her film; see
  [HOT POTATO](https://recursive.eco/view.html?id=162eadff-00fc-4c01-87f7-ec1d2d37f438)); the
  *AI 2027* scenario (Kokotajlo et al., Apr 2025) dates a possible arrival ○, and its authors have
  since moved their dates (dates table).
- **Science lens.** The great filter (§ 14) read forward: if powerful technology is where
  civilizations end, it is also where the odds are decided.
- **Rating yellow.** A question, not a finding.
