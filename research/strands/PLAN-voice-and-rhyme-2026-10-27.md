## Strand 1: the voice record (`NOTES-voice-record.md`)

The seed: one difference people point to between two political leaders is how many of their own
allies break with them in public. A costly break from inside is evidence an outsider cannot easily
fake. The strand turns that into a heuristic for the AI landscape: who has left, or spoken against,
the side they gained from, at what cost, and with what argument.

## Strand 2: the Roman rhyme (`NOTES-roman-rhyme.md`)

The seed: we may be living a transition that rhymes with the end of the Western Roman Empire and
the slow move into what is called feudalism. The strand asks how the rhyme could hold, where it
breaks, and what it suggests to watch for.

## Where they meet

Feudal custom had a word for a public, formal break: *diffidatio*, the renunciation of fealty that
turned resistance from treason into a recognised act (see the Roman note). If the transition rhymes,
the voice record is the modern diffidatio: the record of who renounced fealty, and why. One piece of
work could hold both: a timeline of the new lords, with the breaks marked on it.
