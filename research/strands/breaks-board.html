How to read it

What was broken. A promise means the person said the president did the opposite of what he had said he would do. A policy choice is a disagreement with a decision he never promised otherwise. Conduct or ethics is about honesty or the law in itself. The colour is the coding made here, from what the person said. Each card says why it was coded that way, and several are open to argument.

How it happened. Leaving and saying why costs the most and is the strongest signal. Being pushed out first weakens it, since grievance is possible. Speaking from inside, by vote or letter, spreads the cost. Plea-deal testimony earns the speaker leniency, so it is shown but is not a statement against interest.

What this is not. A score, or a verdict on either president. It covers federal ministers and party members only, and not Lula's third term or Bolsonaro after 2022. Marks: ✔ checked on Oct 27 2026 (the source is named), ◇ not yet checked.
