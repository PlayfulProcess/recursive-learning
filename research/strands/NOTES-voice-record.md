# The voice record: public breaks from inside, as a heuristic (seed, Oct 27 2026)

Marks: ✔ checked on the date given (where it was checked is named) · ◇ recalled, not yet checked.

## The heuristic

When a person who gains from being close to an actor breaks with them in public, and pays for it,
the break is a signal that is hard to fake. It is a heuristic, not a measure of morality: it says
something about the actor, something about the person who broke, and something about the room
they both stood in.

## Ideas it rests on

| Idea | Where | What it lends | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exit, voice and loyalty | Albert O. Hirschman, *Exit, Voice, and Loyalty* (1970) | the three moves an insider has: leave, speak up, stay | ◇ |
| Costly signalling | Michael Spence (job-market signalling, 1973); Amotz Zahavi (handicap principle, 1975) | a signal is believed when it costs the sender | ◇ |
| Declaration against interest | a hearsay exception in US evidence law (Federal Rule of Evidence 804(b)(3)) | courts give more weight to a statement that harms its speaker | ◇ |
| Parties as gatekeepers | Steven Levitsky and Daniel Ziblatt, *How Democracies Die* (2018) | allies who distance themselves from an extremist on their own side are a guardrail | ✔ the argument, from summaries (Wikipedia; LitCharts ch. 5), Oct 27 2026; the book itself not opened |
| Diffidatio | medieval feudal custom | a formal renunciation of fealty that made later resistance legitimate rather than treason | ✔ Cambridge, *Treason and Masculinity in Medieval England*, chapter abstract; Merriam-Webster "diffidation", Oct 27 2026 |

## How to read it (the cautions that make it a heuristic)

- **Few breaks** can mean integrity, or fear and tight control. Silence reads two ways.
- **Many breaks** can mean wrongdoing, or a large and loose coalition, or people leaving to sell a
  book or start a rival.
- **The size of the side matters.** Count against how many people were close, not in the raw.
- **The press picks.** A break in English, in a big outlet, is seen; others are not.
- **Read the argument, not the count.** The record keeps what each person said was wrong. Two
  breaks for opposite reasons are not the same signal.

## What one event records

`who` · `date` · `actor` they broke with · `tie` (employee, founder, minister, board, ally) ·
`move` (exit, voice inside then out, exit with voice) · `cost` (equity, office, career, legal risk) ·
`channel` (post, letter, filing, testimony, interview) · `argument` (in under 20 words of their own,
or paraphrased) · `source` (their own words first) · `checked`.
