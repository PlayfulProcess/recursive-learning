2027 · contact, odds not paths · a draft

2027

Contact with non-human intelligence, of two kinds: the extraterrestrial kind that several traditions expect around 2027, and the AI kind that we may already be living through. Here each claim is attributed and dated, rated by its evidence, and set beside the science and the scholarly criticism.

Odds, not paths. Nothing here says contact will happen in 2027, or that it will not. Each card names what would raise or lower the odds, and asks what you would do either way.

Green documented and checkable Yellow open: said or proposed, not tested Red tested against the record, not borne out The colour judges the claim, not the person. The reasons and sources (opens GitHub in a new tab).

The scholarly criticism

7 Whose Monuments? Green · documented Scholars point to a pattern: ancient-astronaut readings attach far more often to monuments built by non-European peoples (Egypt, Mesoamerica, Great Zimbabwe, Nazca) than to Greek or Roman ones. 1892 · 1929 · 2018
