As-If

Cast face down, forecast before you look, then hold the cards as if true and take one step. It plays the As-If deck, whose cards are read upright or reversed.

Honestly, first: the draw is a shuffle and a coin, so nothing on this page predicts anything. The forecast is yours, written before the coin is shown, and “I don't know” is a legal answer, recorded as itself. Nothing is staked: no points, and no score for being right. What you write stays in this browser unless you download it.
Where the deck leans: it was written from one side of the AI debate. A reviewer found that several of its faces lean toward slowing down or stopping, and that no face yet makes the case for going faster at full strength. Read it as a practice deck, not a survey; a stronger counter-face is welcome.

One Structure, one or two Possibilities, one Process: three cards, because three fits in a body.
