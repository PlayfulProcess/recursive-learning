The art on the cards

    The step of faith is a stag hunt. Step together moves the world only if coordination has reached the bar on the Node; below it the step is spent and a line says so. Rousseau's hunters take a stag together or a hare alone (Skyrms, The Stag Hunt, 2004).

    A model can be fully specified, deterministic, and still fail to show the effect its author believed in. This one is a teaching instrument and nothing more. Its every constant is on the Inheritance cards.
