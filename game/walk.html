The WalkConditional probability, made into steps you actually take.

A probability is not a property of the world. It is a property of the world given a path. Ten percent now, because of how people expect decisions will be made — but at 100% power and 0% alignment the ten becomes something else, and at 100% alignment before the power grows it becomes something else again. Allocate, step, and watch which number moves, and why. Nothing is sent anywhere; the walk lives in the address bar.

Priors — every constant the walk depends on, and not one of them a measurement

The step of faith is a stag hunt. Step together moves the world only if coordination has reached stagK; below it the step is spent, and a line says so. Rousseau's hunters take a stag together or a hare alone: the stag is worth more and nobody can take it alone, so stepping first while the others take hares is the worst cell on the board — see Brian Skyrms, The Stag Hunt and the Evolution of Social Structure (Cambridge, 2004). The collective prayer is the synchronised step.

A model can be fully specified, deterministic, and still fail to show the effect its author believed in. This one is a teaching instrument and nothing more.
