Ekho, at the table

Seven hollows hold beads. The tide rises, the clock speeds up, and the world is hidden under veils. Your moves shift beads toward better hollows. When the tide reaches the Crossing, one bead is drawn from the bag.

    How a round goes

    A round

    • Conditions are drawn: one a round at first, up to three when the clock runs fast.
    • The Council votes. Each player picks Together or Build. With enough Together it passes, and Stop together opens.
    • Moves: each player makes two, from a hand of four cards.
    • The pour: new capability pours into Adolescence. It holds five; the rest spills into Lock-in and Doom.
    • The tide rises one, or two if most voted Build. It holds if half the table (at least two) pledged Stop together in a round the Council passed.

    The board

    • Grace, The turn and The Stop are good hollows. Lock-in and Doom are bad. Adolescence and Still walking are open.
    • Most moves move beads. Build adds one to Adolescence, and Take adds one to Lock-in when The turn is empty; the world adds the rest.
    • At each ◇ mark a veil lifts and the clock speeds up: a round goes from a quarter to a month, a week, a day.
    • Each hidden switch has a gentle and a harsh face. Leads prepare for them: keep Alignment at or above the tide, Relationality at 3 or more, Commons at 2 or more.
    • At the Crossing every veil lifts and one bead is drawn. Its hollow is the ending.

    Roles

      Change role by spending a move, or for free at a Crossroads.
