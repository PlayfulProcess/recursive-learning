Recursive Learning · the ad, playableHot Potato, with others

The AI race is a hot potato: each lab builds faster because the others might. Can you get everyone to hold it instead?

Six others sit in the ring with you. Each follows one rule, written under the ring, and moves after you, one at a time. Tossing it on grows your own lab fastest; the heat belongs to everyone. Holding it together counts only when most of the ring reaches for it and there is enough trust.

A teaching instrument, not a forecast. The numbers come from a small model with its assumptions in the open. Nothing staked, nothing sent. "I don't know" is always a legal move.

Play alone (for careful readers): the same potato with only your hand, and more room to read.

Who sits in the ring, and their rules

"Held" below means held it or held it together. Each lab starts the same size; a lab grows with what its seat puts into building (tossing grows it most). The heat and the trust belong to the whole ring.

    A walk ends when the potato is planted (set down together long enough), when it burns, when it gets through it, still hot (built faster than it was understood, and survived), when one lab keeps it and the trust has gone, or after twenty years still in the air.
