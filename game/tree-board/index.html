The Tree

A cooperative board game on the belief tree. 2–5 players, pass-and-play on one device. Place every character before the race runs out — there is no other way to win or lose.

How to play

Each player is dealt one real person's card. On your turn, your character crosses the next node of the tree (gate, then alignment, then containment) by their own answer. When their answer there is not a plain yes or no, you choose how to cross: cast a coin, cast yarrow, or say "I don't know" (which leaves it open and ends your turn without moving).

Anyone may spend their turn playing one evidence card on any character instead of moving. It can flip a still-open answer freely. It can only flip a settled (plain) answer when the flip would send that character to a leaf they themselves have named as a condition for moving — persuasion on their own terms, never invented.

The race ticks once every time the turn returns to the first player, and once more the moment any character first reaches Proceed. The table wins if, before the race fills, everyone lands on one shared leaf, or every remaining split between leaves has been named (tap the node, or the button, where two characters part ways). It loses if the race fills first. No verdict is given — only what happened.
